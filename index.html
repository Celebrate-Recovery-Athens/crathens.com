New to Celebrate Recovery?

It's a Christ-centered recovery program for hurts, hang-ups, and habits. You don't have to label yourself as anything, or say a word, to attend.

There's no cost and no membership. Open share groups are separated by gender, and what's said in the room stays in the room.

Anyone is welcome, whatever church you attend or don't.

For churches

We're an established group with trained leaders, currently seeking a host church in the Athens area. We need one evening a week — a large room plus a few smaller ones — and we're flexible on which night.

Celebrate Recovery is a ministry of the local church. We operate under the Celebrate Recovery DNA and under the oversight of our host church, and we're in contact with our regional Celebrate Recovery representative.

If your church has an evening free, we'd be glad to talk. Reach Phyllis at info@crathens.com.
