New to Celebrate Recovery?

It's a Christ-centered recovery program for hurts, hang-ups, and habits. You don't have to label yourself as anything, or say a word, to attend.

There's no cost and no membership. Open share groups are separated by gender, and what's said in the room stays in the room.

Anyone is welcome, whatever church you attend or don't.
